A Festival That Lights Up Every Heart

Diwali is the festival of lights, when thousands of small flames rise against the dark and remind us that hope is always stronger than the night.

Homes are cleaned and painted, doorways are drawn with rangoli, sweets travel from kitchen to kitchen, and families gather around the glow of a single diya.

It celebrates the triumph of light over darkness, of knowledge over ignorance, and of love over everything that divides us.
